About

About Us

Your Local Market is a non profit organization that works together with Farmer Markets and NAMI (National Alliance on Mental Illness) to build healthier communities. Our goal is to improve the physical and mental health of every American. We will be providing quality and affordable food accessible to everyone, as well as fighting the stigma surrounding mental health by bringing awareness and providing access to mental health professionals.

- Healing our communities one neighborhood at a time

Our Services

Fresh Food

We work with every Farmer Market in the country to guarantee access to the best quality of food. We provide farmers with a space to provide for the communities as well as grow their business.

Mental Health

We partner with the National Alliance on Mental Illness to install booths on each of our markets. These booths are run by mental health professionals and filled with resources to support the needs of the community.

Community Support

We want to improve the quality of life of every American, which is why every vendor accepts SNAP and EBT benefits.
